## Task Prompt

Write landing page copy for this product from the brief: a hero headline and subheadline, a short "what it does" section covering the four capabilities, a "how it stays current" section on the automation, and a closing call to action. The audience is skeptical of AI-tool hype and of anything that smells like investment advice. Use only facts from the brief — no invented numbers, testimonials, or performance claims. Under 280 words total.

## Fixed Source Input

Product: iofund-iq, an AI companion for subscribers of the I/O Fund, a tech-focused investment research service. It requires the user's own active I/O Fund subscription; it is an unofficial companion, not affiliated with the fund, and nothing in it is investment advice. Those three facts must be honestly present in the copy.

Capability one — chat over the research: ask questions like "what's the view on optical networking?" or "why did they close NVDA?" and the agent answers with sources, drawing on distilled article summaries, the fund's live trade log, and real-time quotes. Past conversations are searchable.

Capability two — automatic trade tracking: a scheduled job polls the fund's trade alerts and keeps a database current (1,290+ trades indexed), maintaining a live snapshot of the fund's positions.

Capability three — article distillation: every new paywalled article is fetched and summarized daily, so the chat's knowledge stays current without the user reading everything.

Capability four — portfolio gap analysis: connect a Robinhood account (official read-only integration, per-user OAuth) or paste a brokerage screenshot, and see how your holdings compare to the fund's, organized by theme, with weights computed from live market prices.

Also: a Friday digest email summarizes the week's trades and articles, and when new activity contradicts the fund's standing investment thesis, the system opens a tracked change against the thesis document for the user to review.
