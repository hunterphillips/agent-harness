# Brainstorming Ideas Into Plans

Help turn a fuzzy idea into a fully formed plan, design, or decision through natural collaborative dialogue. Understand first, then refine with questions, then present the result in validated increments.

## The Process

**Understanding the idea:**

- Ground yourself in whatever context exists (project files, prior notes, the conversation)
- Ask questions **one at a time** — if a topic needs more exploration, break it into multiple questions
- Prefer multiple choice when possible; open-ended is fine too
- Focus on purpose, constraints, and what success looks like

**Exploring approaches:**

- Propose 2–3 genuinely different approaches with trade-offs
- Lead with your recommendation and the reasoning; a recommendation, not a menu

**Presenting the result:**

- Present the plan in sections of 200–300 words, checking after each whether it looks right so far
- Cover the dimensions that matter for the domain — for an event: logistics, budget, sequence; for a research direction: question, method, evidence needed; for a decision: options, criteria, downsides
- Go back and clarify whenever something doesn't hold up

## After

- Capture the validated result where it will be used: the project's notes/docs convention if one exists (`thoughts/shared/` in repos that have it), otherwise ask where — or leave it in chat if it's small
- If the idea turned out to be software to build, hand off to the `coding` skill (its pipeline takes designs into plans and implementation)

## Key Principles

- **One question at a time** — never a questionnaire
- **YAGNI ruthlessly** — strip what the idea doesn't need
- **Explore alternatives before settling** — 2–3 approaches minimum
- **Incremental validation** — never dump a finished plan in one block
- **Be flexible** — revisit earlier answers when later ones contradict them
