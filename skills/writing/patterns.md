# Pattern catalogue

The long tail of AI tells, for long pieces, publication drafts, or when the eight structural tells in [humanizer.md](humanizer.md) don't account for what still reads as generated. Those eight are not repeated here. Based on [Wikipedia:Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing).

## Content

### Promotional language

boasts, vibrant, rich (figurative), profound, nestled, in the heart of, groundbreaking, renowned, breathtaking, stunning. Models lose neutrality on "cultural heritage" topics in particular.

> **Before:** Nestled within the breathtaking Gonder region, Alamata Raya Kobo stands as a vibrant town with a rich cultural heritage.
> **After:** Alamata Raya Kobo is a town in the Gonder region of Ethiopia, known for its weekly market and 18th-century church.

### Notability claims and media lists

Sources listed without content: "cited in The New York Times, BBC, and Financial Times," "active social media presence."

> **Before:** Her views have been cited in The New York Times, BBC, Financial Times, and The Hindu.
> **After:** In a 2024 New York Times interview, she argued that AI regulation should focus on outcomes rather than methods.

### Vague attribution

Industry reports, observers have cited, experts argue, some critics say. Name the source or drop the claim.

> **Before:** Experts believe the river plays a crucial role in the regional ecosystem.
> **After:** The river supports several endemic fish species, according to a 2019 survey by the Chinese Academy of Sciences.

### Formulaic "challenges" sections

"Despite its X, it faces challenges… Despite these challenges, it continues to thrive."

> **Before:** Despite its prosperity, Korattur faces challenges typical of urban areas. Despite these challenges, Korattur continues to thrive.
> **After:** Traffic congestion increased after 2015 when three new IT parks opened. The municipal corporation began a drainage project in 2022.

### Generic positive conclusions

"The future looks bright," "exciting times lie ahead," "a major step in the right direction." End on a fact.

> **Before:** The future looks bright as they continue their journey toward excellence.
> **After:** The company plans to open two more locations next year.

### Persuasive authority tropes

"The real question is," "at its core," "what really matters," "fundamentally," "the deeper issue." Ceremony before an ordinary point. Delete the frame and state the claim.

> **Before:** At its core, what really matters is organizational readiness.
> **After:** That mostly depends on whether the organization is ready to change its habits.

### Stock idioms and business clichés

heavy lifting, move the needle, low-hanging fruit, at the end of the day, game changer, hit the ground running, circle back. Replace with the specific thing that is happening.

> **Before:** The caching layer really moves the needle on latency.
> **After:** The caching layer cuts median latency from 400ms to 90ms.

## Language

### AI vocabulary

align with, crucial, delve, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, interplay, intricate, landscape (abstract), pivotal, showcase, tapestry, testament, underscore, vibrant, robust, throughline, load-bearing (outside construction).

Not a banned-word list. Most are ordinary English in ordinary use. Two things raise them to a tell: they cluster, and they appear in the inflated or abstract sense (a landscape of ideas, a testament to resilience). The list dates fast; the structural tells are the reliable signal.

> **Before:** An enduring testament to Italian influence is the adoption of pasta in the local culinary landscape.
> **After:** Pasta dishes, introduced during Italian colonization, remain common, especially in the south.

### Elegant variation

Synonym cycling from repetition penalties: the protagonist, the main character, the central figure, the hero. Repeat the word.

### False ranges

"From X to Y" where X and Y are not on a scale.

> **Before:** From the singularity of the Big Bang to the enigmatic dance of dark matter.
> **After:** The book covers the Big Bang, star formation, and current theories about dark matter.

### Subjectless fragments and hidden actors

"No configuration file needed." "The results are preserved automatically." Restore the subject when active voice is clearer.

> **After:** You do not need a configuration file. The system preserves the results.

### Filler phrases

in order to → to · due to the fact that → because · at this point in time → now · in the event that → if · has the ability to → can · it is important to note that → (delete)

### Hedging stacks

"Could potentially possibly be argued that." Often the right number of qualifiers is zero; when one is warranted, one carries it. Do not overcorrect into false certainty: a single honest "we don't know X" is good writing, and a qualifier that sets the claim's strength stays (humanizer.md, task step 4). Genuinely open questions go in their own list, not woven through the prose as caveats.

### Hyphenated pair uniformity

third-party, cross-functional, data-driven, high-quality, real-time, end-to-end, hyphenated with perfect consistency. Humans are inconsistent; technical compound modifiers are fine.

## Formatting and punctuation

Any one of these is fine. The tell is density.

- **Em dashes** as sentence glue where a comma, period, or "because" belongs.
- **Colon chains**, "claim: elaboration" at every joint. Colons still introduce a list, a definition, or a counted enumeration. Fix order: own sentence, then comma appositive, then "because" or "so."
- **Boldface** on phrases mechanically, and **inline-header lists** ("- **Speed:** queries are faster") where a sentence would carry it.
- **Markdown in flowing prose**: headers, bullets, bolded lead-ins in an essay, report, or message. Lists are for content that is a list.
- **Fragmented headers**: a heading followed by a one-line restatement of the heading before the real content.
- **Title Case In Headings**, **emojis** on bullets, **curly quotes**.

> **Before:** The effect is real but bounded: reorganizing the input yields only modest gains. The reason is simple: the model already reads the whole record.
> **After:** The effect is real but bounded. Reorganizing the input yields only modest gains because the model already reads the whole record.

## Chatbot artifacts

Correspondence pasted as content, disclaimers left in, and people-pleasing.

- "I hope this helps," "Certainly!", "Would you like…," "Let me know if…"
- "As of [date]," "while specific details are limited," "based on available information"
- "Great question!", "You're absolutely right," "That's an excellent point"

> **Before:** Great question! While specific details are scarce, the company appears to have been established in the 1990s. I hope this helps!
> **After:** The company was founded in 1994, according to its registration documents.
